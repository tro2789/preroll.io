preroll.io is the podcast production management platform built for service providers. If you produce podcasts for clients — whether you're a solo editor or a multi-person agency — preroll.io consolidates your episode pipelines, client communication, asset organization, and publishing into one place.

preroll.io is **API-first**: the REST API is the product, and every interface (web UI, client portal, MCP server) is a consumer of the same API. It's an orchestration layer that connects your existing tools — Frame.io, Google Drive, Vimeo, YouTube, Transistor.fm — rather than replacing them.

## Who is preroll.io for?

- **Solo podcast producers** managing multiple client shows
- **Production agencies** coordinating teams across dozens of active episodes
- **Self-hosters** who want full control over their production workflow
