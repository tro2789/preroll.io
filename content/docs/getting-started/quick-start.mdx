# Quick Start

This guide walks you through creating your first client, show, and episode in PreRoll. By the end, you'll have a working production pipeline ready for real work.

**Time required:** ~5 minutes

---

## Step 1: Create Your Account

1. Go to [preroll.io](https://preroll.io) and click **Get Started**.
2. Sign up with your email (magic link) or Google account.
3. PreRoll automatically creates your **organization** — this is your workspace where all data lives.

<Callout type="info">
  All new accounts get a **7-day Studio trial** with full access to every feature, including multi-user, white-label, and reporting.
</Callout>

---

## Step 2: Create Your First Client

Clients represent the people or companies you produce podcasts for.

1. From the dashboard, click **New Client** (or navigate to **Clients** in the sidebar).
2. Fill in the basics:
   - **Name** — the client's name or company
   - **Email** — their contact email
   - **Company** — optional, useful for agency work
3. Click **Create Client**.

You can add more details later — meeting notes, service terms, company info — but a name and email are enough to start.

---

## Step 3: Create a Show

Shows belong to a client and represent an individual podcast series.

1. Open your new client's page.
2. Click **New Show**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Name** — the podcast title
   - **Format** — interview, solo, narrative, etc.
   - **Schedule** — weekly, biweekly, etc.
4. Click **Create Show**.

PreRoll creates a default pipeline for your show:

```
Planning → Recording → Editing → Review → Approved → Published
```

You can customize these stages later from the show's settings.

---

## Step 4: Add an Episode

Episodes are the core unit of work in PreRoll. Each one moves through your show's pipeline stages.

1. Open the show you just created.
2. Click **New Episode** (or use the **+** button on the kanban board).
3. Enter:
   - **Title** — the episode title
   - **Episode number** — auto-incremented if left blank
   - **Description** — optional notes or show notes
4. Click **Create Episode**.

Your episode appears in the first pipeline stage (Planning). Drag it across the board as work progresses, or click into it to update details, upload assets, and manage deliverables.

---

## Step 5: Invite a Team Member (Studio Plan)

If you're on the Studio plan (or during your trial), you can add team members to your organization.

1. Go to **Settings** → **Team**.
2. Click **Invite Member**.
3. Enter their email and select a role:
   - **Admin** — full access, can manage billing and team
   - **Member** — can manage clients, shows, and episodes
4. Click **Send Invite**.

They'll receive a magic link email to join your organization.

<Callout type="warn">
  Team members are a Studio plan feature. After your trial ends, you'll need to upgrade to Studio ($79/month) to keep multi-user access.
</Callout>
