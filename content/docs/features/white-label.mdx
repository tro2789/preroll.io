# White-Label Branding

White-label branding lets you customize the client portal so it reflects your brand instead of PreRoll's. Clients interact with a portal that looks and feels like your own product — building trust and presenting a polished, professional experience.

{/* Screenshot: White-Label Portal — /docs/screenshots/white-label.png */}

## What You Can Customize

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Logo | Your company logo displayed in the portal header and login page |
| Accent color | Primary color used for buttons, links, and highlights |
| Display name | Your business name shown in the portal header and emails |

### Logo

- Appears in the portal navigation bar
- Displayed on the magic-link login page
- Used in email notifications to clients
- Recommended size: 200x50px (horizontal/landscape format)
- Supported formats: PNG, SVG, JPG
- Transparent background recommended

### Accent Color

The accent color is applied to:

- Primary buttons (Approve, Submit, etc.)
- Links and interactive elements
- Active/selected state indicators
- Progress bars and status badges

Choose a color that matches your brand. PreRoll ensures sufficient contrast for accessibility.

### Display Name

Your business name replaces "PreRoll" throughout the client-facing experience:

- Portal header: "Powered by [Your Name]" becomes just your name
- Email sender name
- Browser tab title
- Login page welcome text

## Configuration

### Setting Up White-Label

1. Navigate to **Settings > Branding**
2. Upload your logo
3. Select your accent color (hex picker or presets)
4. Enter your display name
5. Click **Save**

Changes take effect immediately for all client portal sessions.

{/* Screenshot: Branding Settings — /docs/screenshots/white-label-settings.png */}

### Preview

Before saving, use the preview panel to see how your branding looks:

- Login page preview
- Portal dashboard preview
- Email notification preview
- Mobile view preview

## Per-Organization Branding

White-label settings are configured per organization. This means:

- Each organization has its own branding
- Clients of different organizations see different branding
- Team members within an organization share the same branding settings
- Only owners and admins can modify branding settings

## Where Branding Appears

### Client Portal

| Location | Branding Applied |
|----------|-----------------|
| Login page | Logo, accent color, display name |
| Navigation header | Logo, display name |
| Buttons & links | Accent color |
| Status badges | Accent color variants |
| Footer | Display name |

### Email Notifications

| Element | Branding Applied |
|---------|-----------------|
| Header logo | Your uploaded logo |
| Sender name | Your display name |
| Button color | Your accent color |
| Footer text | Your display name |

### Review Player

The review player inherits your accent color for:

- Play button and progress bar
- Comment submission buttons
- Approve/revision action buttons

## Client Experience

With white-label branding configured, your clients:

- See your brand when they access the portal
- Receive emails that appear to come from your business
- Never see "PreRoll" branding in their interface
- Get a cohesive experience that matches your other touchpoints

## Default Branding

If you don't configure white-label branding:

- The portal uses PreRoll's default styling
- Emails are sent as "PreRoll" with default branding
- The portal header shows "PreRoll"

## Best Practices

- **Use a horizontal logo** — Vertical/square logos don't fit well in navigation bars
- **Choose a distinctive accent color** — Not too similar to the background or text colors
- **Keep your display name short** — It appears in many places with limited space
- **Test on mobile** — Check how your branding looks on smaller screens
- **Update branding if you rebrand** — Changes take effect immediately across all client sessions
