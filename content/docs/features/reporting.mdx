## Available Reports

### Episodes Produced Over Time

A line chart showing how many episodes you've completed (reached "Published" status) over time.

- **Daily, weekly, or monthly** granularity
- **Trend line** showing whether production volume is increasing or decreasing
- **Comparison** — Current period vs. previous period
- **Total count** for the selected time range

### On-Time Delivery Rate

Percentage of episodes that were completed by their deadline:

```
On-Time Rate = (Episodes delivered on or before deadline / Episodes with deadlines) x 100
```

- Displayed as a percentage with a trend indicator
- Broken down by month to show improvement or regression
- Episodes without deadlines are excluded from this calculation
- "On time" means the episode reached "Published" or "Approved" by the deadline date

### Approval Turnaround Time

How long it takes clients to review and approve deliverables:

- **Average turnaround** — Mean time from submission to approval
- **Median turnaround** — Less affected by outliers (one client sitting on a review for weeks)
- **By client** — See which clients are fast vs. slow to approve
- **Trend** — Whether turnaround is getting faster or slower

### Pipeline Velocity

How quickly episodes move through your production pipeline:

- Average time spent in each stage
- Stages where episodes tend to get stuck
- Total time from creation to publication
- Comparison across shows

## Breakdowns

## Report Cards

The reporting page displays key metrics as summary cards at the top:

- **Total episodes** published in the selected period
- **On-time rate** as a percentage
- **Avg. turnaround** for client approvals
- **Active shows** that produced episodes in the period

Each card includes a comparison to the previous equivalent period (e.g., last month vs. the month before).

## Charts and Visualization

Reports include interactive charts:

- **Line charts** — Trends over time (episodes produced, turnaround)
- **Bar charts** — Comparisons by show or client
- **Donut charts** — Distribution of episodes by stage or status
- **Heatmaps** — Activity density by day of week / time of day

Hover over any data point for detailed numbers.

## Use Cases

### Client Reporting

Share production metrics with clients:

- "We delivered 12 episodes this quarter, 92% on time"
- "Average turnaround from your approval request to delivery: 1.4 days"
- Useful for contract renewals and demonstrating value

### Internal Optimization

Identify bottlenecks in your workflow:

- If episodes spend 5 days in "Editing" but only 1 day in "Recording," you might need more editing capacity
- If one show consistently misses deadlines, investigate why
- If turnaround times are increasing, you may be approaching capacity

### Capacity Planning

Use trends to plan ahead:

- Production volume trends help predict when you'll need to hire
- Client-by-client breakdown shows where growth is coming from
- Seasonal patterns help you plan vacation and coverage

## Tips

- **Set deadlines consistently** — The on-time rate is only meaningful if most episodes have deadlines
- **Check monthly** — Monthly review cadence gives you enough data for meaningful trends
- **Share with clients** — Proactive reporting builds trust and justifies your rates
- **Export before meetings** — Have concrete numbers ready for client check-ins
