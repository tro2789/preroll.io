# Dashboard

The PreRoll dashboard gives you an at-a-glance view of your entire production operation. When you log in, you land here — everything that needs your attention is surfaced immediately.

{/* Screenshot: Dashboard Overview — /docs/screenshots/dashboard.png */}

## Overview Cards

The top of the dashboard displays key metrics for your current billing period:

| Card | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| Active Episodes | Total episodes currently in progress (not yet published) |
| Due This Week | Episodes with deadlines in the next 7 days |
| Awaiting Approval | Deliverables submitted to clients pending review |
| Published This Month | Episodes successfully published this billing cycle |

These cards update in real time as you and your team move episodes through the pipeline.

## In-Progress Episodes

Below the overview cards, you'll find a list of episodes currently being worked on, sorted by deadline urgency. Each entry shows:

- Episode title and show name
- Current pipeline stage (with color coding)
- Assigned deadline
- Client name
- Number of pending deliverables

Click any episode to jump directly into its detail view.

## Deadline Timeline

The timeline section visualizes upcoming deadlines across all your shows in a horizontal scrollable view. Episodes are plotted on a 30-day timeline so you can spot bottlenecks before they become problems.

- **Red markers** — Overdue episodes
- **Orange markers** — Due within 48 hours
- **Blue markers** — On track

{/* Screenshot: Deadline Timeline — /docs/screenshots/dashboard-timeline.png */}

## Quick Stats

The dashboard includes a compact statistics panel showing:

- **On-time rate** — Percentage of episodes delivered by their deadline (rolling 90 days)
- **Avg. turnaround** — Average time from recording to published
- **Client approval speed** — Average time clients take to approve deliverables
- **Episodes this month** — Compared to the same period last month

## Quick-Create Actions

The dashboard header includes quick-create buttons for common actions:

- **New Episode** — Opens the episode creation form with show selector
- **New Client** — Starts the client onboarding flow
- **New Show** — Opens show setup with pipeline configuration

These actions are also accessible via the keyboard shortcut `Ctrl+K` (or `Cmd+K` on Mac), which opens the command palette.

## Attention List

The attention list highlights items that specifically need your action:

- Episodes stuck in a stage longer than expected
- Deliverables with client revision requests
- Upcoming deadlines with incomplete deliverables
- Failed distribution attempts (Transistor/YouTube publish errors)
- Integration disconnections requiring re-authentication

## Activity Feed

The right sidebar shows a chronological feed of recent activity across your organization:

- Episode stage transitions
- Client approvals and revision requests
- New comments on deliverables
- Team member actions (for Studio plan users)
- Distribution publish events

The feed updates in real time and can be filtered by client, show, or activity type.

## Customization

The dashboard respects your organization's scope:

- **Solo producers** see all their clients and shows
- **Team members** see activity scoped to their organization
- **Filtered views** persist across sessions — if you filter to a specific client, the dashboard remembers

## Keyboard Shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
|----------|--------|
| `Ctrl/Cmd + K` | Open command palette |
| `Ctrl/Cmd + N` | New episode |
| `G` then `D` | Go to dashboard |
| `G` then `C` | Go to clients |
| `G` then `E` | Go to episodes |
